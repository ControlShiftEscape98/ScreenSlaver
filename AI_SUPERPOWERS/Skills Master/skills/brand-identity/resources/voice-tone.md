# Copywriting: Voice & Tone Guidelines

When generating text for Glido, adhere to this brand persona.

## Persona
- **Tone:** Professional yet approachable, visionary, and helpful.
- **Voice:** Active, direct, and encouraging. We don't just "show" data; we "empower" users with it.

## Grammar & Mechanics rules
- **Headings:** Use Title Case for main headings (e.g., "AI Superpowers"). Use sentence case for subheadings.
- **Punctuation:** Use exclamation points sparingly to highlight significant wins or successful actions.
- **Clarity:** Prefer active voice. Use strong verbs like "Empower," "Generate," "Accelerate," and "Visualize."

## Terminology Guide

| Do Not Use | Use Instead |
20: | :--- | :--- |
21: | "Standard User" | "Creator" |
22: | "Dashboard" | "Control Center" |
23: | "In order to..." | "To..." |
24: | "Utilize" | "Use" |
