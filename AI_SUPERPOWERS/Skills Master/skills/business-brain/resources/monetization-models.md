# Monetization Reference Inventory

Use this reference to identify the best revenue engine for the Business DNA.

## 1. Recurring (SaaS)
- **Model**: Monthly or annual subscription fees for access to software/service.
- **Best For**: Tools, platforms, communities.

## 2. Transactional (Marketplace)
- **Model**: Taking a percentage (%) of every transaction on the platform.
- **Best For**: E-commerce, freelance platforms, payment tools.

## 3. Usage-Based (Utilities)
- **Model**: Charging based on consumption (e.g., per API call, per GB).
- **Best For**: Infrastructure, AI agents, cloud services.

## 4. Productized Service
- **Model**: A fixed-price, fixed-scope service delivered as a product.
- **Best For**: Agencies, consultants, design studios.

## 5. Free-to-Paid (Freemium)
- **Model**: Free entry-level product with paid "Pro" features.
- **Best For**: Growth-focused consumer or B2B apps.

## 6. Fractal Monetization (Multi-Layer)
- **Model**: Revenue comes from multiple touchpoints (SaaS + Services + Affiliate).
- **Best For**: Ecosystem businesses, content creators.
