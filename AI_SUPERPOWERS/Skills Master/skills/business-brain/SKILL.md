# Business Brain (Strategic Reasoning Engine)

This skill transforms technical projects into viable businesses by defining their "Business DNA" and "Maker Identity" and architecting their integration into larger ecosystems.

## 🚀 The Strategic Workflow

### 1. Step 1: Genesis (Business DNA)
Initialize the project by generating a **`BUSINESS_DNA.md`** file using the **[DNA Template](./resources/business-dna.md)** and **[The Maker Template](./resources/the-maker.md)**.
- Gather data for "The Machine" (Target, Problem, Journey, Metrics).
- Gather data for "The Maker" (Story, Credibility, Edge).

### 2. Step 2: Monetization Architecture
Analyze the user's project against the **[Monetization Models](./resources/monetization-models.md)**.
- Select the best revenue engine (SaaS, Transactional, etc.).
- Define the "Economics of the Machine."

### 3. Step 3: Fractal Growth
Use the **[Fractal Architecture](./resources/fractal-architecture.md)** guidelines to design how this project scales.
- How does it operate independently?
- How does it slot into a bigger business layout?

---

## 📋 Business System Initialization Loop

When starting a new business strategy task, follow this loop:

1.  **Identity Check**: "Let's define the Business DNA. Can you describe your ideal customer in 1-2 sharp sentences?"
2.  **Logic Mapping**: Cross-reference the problem with the [Monetization Reference](./resources/monetization-models.md).
3.  **Synthesis**: Generate the first draft of the `BUSINESS_DNA.md` at the project root.
4.  **Verification**: Conduct a "Reality Check" on the Bottlenecks and Metrics.
