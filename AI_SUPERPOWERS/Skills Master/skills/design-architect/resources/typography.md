# Dimension 4: Typography (The Voice)

Strategic font pairings based on brand personality.

## 🚀 Modern/Tech
- **Headings:** Inter (Variable)
- **Body:** Roboto or System UI
- **Mono:** JetBrains Mono
- **Weights:** 400 (regular), 600 (semibold), 700 (bold)

## 🎩 Elegant/Luxury
- **Headings:** Playfair Display
- **Body:** Montserrat
- **Accents:** Cormorant Garamond
- **Weights:** 300 (light), 400 (regular), 700 (bold)

## 😊 Friendly/Consumer
- **Headings:** Poppins
- **Body:** Open Sans
- **Weights:** 400 (regular), 600 (semibold), 800 (extrabold)

## 🧱 Brutalist/Bold
- **Headings:** Space Grotesk
- **Body:** IBM Plex Sans
- **Weights:** 400 (regular), 700 (bold)

## 📰 Editorial/Content
- **Headings:** Merriweather
- **Body:** Source Sans Pro
- **Weights:** 300 (light), 400 (regular), 700 (bold)
