## 🛠️ Color System Checklist
- **60-30-10 Rule:** Maintain color balance.
- **Accessibility:** Ensure 4.5:1 ratio for standard text (7:1 for premium).
- **Off-Blacks:** Avoid pure `#000000`; use `#0A0A0A` or `#1E293B`.
- **Semantic Tokens:** Always define `--bg`, `--surface`, `--border`, `--text`, `--cta`.
